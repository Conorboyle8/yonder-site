Support

Need a hand or found a bug? Email hello.yonder.app@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app.

How do I use Yonder?

Swipe right to add a place to today, left to pass, and up to save it for another time. Tap a card to flip it for more details. Your picks are in the Today tab, where you can open them in Maps.

Why am I seeing places far away, or none at all?

Yonder shows places within your maximum distance, which you can change in Settings. If location is turned off for Yonder, it measures from central London. To turn it on, open your iPhone's Settings, then Yonder, then Location.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional, and backs up your lists so you can get them back on a new phone. You sign in with a code sent to your email, so there's no password.

Why did my list reset?

Yonder is about today, so each new day starts with a fresh list and deck. Places you saved for another time stay in the Today tab.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You need to be signed in. See the privacy policy for exactly what's deleted.
