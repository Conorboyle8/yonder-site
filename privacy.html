Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Yonder is an app for finding things to do near you today. This policy explains what information it uses, why, and the choices you have. Yonder is run by Conor Boyle in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller. Contact: hello.yonder.app@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use Yonder without an account.
  • We use your location to show nearby places. We don't store it.
  • We record how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • We don't sell your information, show third-party ad networks, or track you across other companies' apps.

What we collect and why

Your location

With your permission, Yonder reads your location while the app is open. Your phone uses it to work out how far away places are. To search for places, a position rounded to about 1 km is sent to our server and on to Google. We don't store your location or location history. If you refuse permission, Yonder uses central London instead.

Usage information

We record what happens in the app: which places were shown and how far away they were, how you swiped, how long you looked at a card, and when you open a place in Maps or change a setting. These records include a random ID created when you install the app, a session ID, your app version and device platform. If you're signed in, they're also linked to your account. We use them to understand what works, fix problems and improve suggestions.

Your account (optional)

If you sign in, we keep your email address so we can send you sign-in codes. We also keep your lists, past days, swipes and what the app has learned about your taste, so you can get them back on another phone.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, we keep your message, plus your email address if you choose to leave it, so we can read it and reply.

Stored on your phone

Your list for today, saved places and taste settings are kept on your phone so the app works quickly. This includes details of places you've added, which are removed when they leave your lists.

Our lawful bases

  • Location: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Accounts and syncing: providing the service you asked for.
  • Usage information and feedback: our legitimate interest in running and improving Yonder.

Who we share it with

We use these companies to run Yonder. They handle information on our behalf or provide services the app relies on:

  • Supabase hosts our database and server, in the EU.
  • Google (Google Places) receives your rounded position to find places, and supplies place details and photos. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Wikimedia serves some photos directly to your phone, so it sees your IP address.
  • Expo delivers app updates to your phone.
  • Our email provider delivers sign-in codes to your address.
  • Apple or Google provide your app store and the Maps app you choose to open.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that applies, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires. We don't sell your personal information. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account information until you delete your account. We keep usage information and feedback only for as long as we need them to run and improve Yonder, then delete or anonymise them. Your rounded position isn't stored by us after a search has finished.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, email address, lists, swipes and taste profile. Usage records and feedback are then no longer linked to your account, but they stay linked to your install's random ID. To have those removed too, email hello.yonder.app@gmail.com. Deleting the app clears what's stored on your phone.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct, delete or stop using it, by emailing hello.yonder.app@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Yonder isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Some places shown serve alcohol.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
